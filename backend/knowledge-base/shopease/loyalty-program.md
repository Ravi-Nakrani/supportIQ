# ShopEase Rewards Loyalty Program

## Program Overview and Enrollment
ShopEase Rewards is our complimentary customer loyalty program designed to reward shoppers for their continued patronage. Enrollment is automatic and free for every registered customer account upon placing their first order or signing up on the website. Members earn reward points on every qualified purchase, gain access to exclusive member promotions, and enjoy special milestone perks.

## Earning Points on Purchases
Members earn reward points across all standard product purchases made through ShopEase:
- **Base Earning Rate:** Customers earn 1 reward point for every $1.00 spent on qualifying merchandise (calculated on net item subtotal after applied discounts, excluding taxes and shipping fees).
- **Promotional Multipliers:** ShopEase hosts periodic "Double Points Days" and seasonal flash events where select categories earn 2x or 3x reward points.
- Points are credited to the customer loyalty balance as "Pending" upon order placement and become fully "Available" for redemption 30 days following order delivery (matching the standard return window).

## Redeeming Points and Reward Values
Available reward points can be redeemed directly at checkout as an instant cash discount:
- **Conversion Rate:** 100 reward points equal a $5.00 discount on your order total (each individual point is valued at 5 cents).
- **Redemption Increments:** Points may be redeemed in increments of 100 points (e.g., 100 points for $5 off, 200 points for $10 off, up to 1,000 points for $50 off per transaction).
- Points can be combined with sitewide discount codes and manufacturer promotions, up to a maximum redemption discount covering 50% of the cart merchandise subtotal.

## Points Expiration and Account Activity
ShopEase reward points remain active as long as the member maintains qualifying account activity:
- Points expire 12 months after the last qualifying activity date.
- A "Qualifying Activity" is defined as any merchandise purchase, points redemption, or product review submitted through the verified account.
- Engaging in a qualifying activity automatically extends the expiration date of all active points in the balance for an additional 12 months.
- Customers receive automated email reminder alerts 60 days and 30 days prior to any pending point expiration.

## Membership Tiers and Exclusive Benefits
ShopEase Rewards features three progressive membership tiers determined by annual calendar spend:
- **Silver Tier ($0 to $499 annual spend):** Standard 1 point per $1 spent, early access to seasonal sales, and free standard shipping on orders over $50.
- **Gold Tier ($500 to $999 annual spend):** 1.25 points per $1 spent (25% points bonus), free standard shipping on all orders with no minimum purchase requirement, and an annual $15 birthday reward voucher.
- **Platinum Tier ($1,000+ annual spend):** 1.5 points per $1 spent (50% points bonus), free 2-day Express Shipping on all orders, dedicated VIP priority customer support, and quarterly double-point bonus weekends.

## Program Exclusions and Restrictions
To maintain program integrity, the following transactions and items are excluded from point accrual and redemption:
- Reward points cannot be earned on the purchase of ShopEase Gift Cards, payment of state or local sales taxes, carrier shipping fees, or Care+ extended warranties.
- Reward points have no cash surrender value, cannot be withdrawn as fiat currency, and are non-transferable between separate accounts.
- In the event that an item is returned for a refund, any points earned from that specific item purchase will be deducted from the customer's loyalty balance.
