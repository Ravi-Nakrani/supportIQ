# ShopEase Shipping Policy

## Shipping Methods and Delivery Estimates
ShopEase partners with tier-one domestic logistics carriers (including FedEx, UPS, and USPS) to offer reliable delivery options across all service territories:
- **Standard Shipping:** Delivers within 3 to 5 business days after carrier dispatch. Standard shipping is free on all orders with a merchandise subtotal of $50.00 or more (before taxes and after promotional discounts). For orders below $50.00, a flat shipping fee of $4.99 applies.
- **Express Shipping:** Delivers within 1 to 2 business days after carrier dispatch. Express shipping is available for a flat rate of $14.99 on all orders regardless of cart total. Express delivery requires a physical street address and cannot be delivered to P.O. boxes.

## Order Processing and Dispatch Timelines
Orders are processed and packaged at our fulfillment centers Monday through Friday, excluding official federal holidays:
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST) on a business day are scheduled for dispatch on the same day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST, on weekends, or during federal holidays are processed and dispatched on the following business day.
- During peak seasonal promotional events (e.g., Black Friday, Cyber Week), order handling may take up to an additional 24 hours prior to carrier handoff.

## Service Areas and Delivery Restrictions
ShopEase currently ships to addresses within the fifty United States and the District of Columbia:
- Deliveries to Alaska and Hawaii are serviced via Standard Air freight and typically require 5 to 7 business days for final arrival.
- We do not currently service international addresses, APO/FPO/DPO military addresses, or freight-forwarding hubs. Orders directed to known freight forwarders will be automatically cancelled for security and fraud prevention.

## Tracking and Delivery Notifications
Once an order has been verified, packed, and handed over to the carrier:
- An automated shipment confirmation email is immediately dispatched containing the assigned carrier name, tracking number, and direct tracking URL.
- Tracking information typically updates in the carrier's digital tracking portal within 12 to 24 hours of package scanning.
- Customers can track real-time parcel status directly within their ShopEase account under the "Track Shipment" section.

## Lost, Damaged, or Delayed Shipments
ShopEase provides complete coverage for items lost or damaged in transit:
- If a package shows no tracking movement for seven consecutive business days, customers should contact ShopEase Support to initiate a formal carrier trace. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, a direct replacement will be dispatched via Express Shipping at no extra cost, or a full refund will be issued.
- If a shipment arrives damaged, notify ShopEase Support within 48 hours of delivery with packaging photos to initiate a rapid replacement or full refund.
