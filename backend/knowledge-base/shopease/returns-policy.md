# ShopEase Returns Policy

## Overview and Return Window
At ShopEase, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We offer a standard 30-day return window for most physical products purchased through the ShopEase platform. The 30-day period begins on the calendar day the carrier marks the order as delivered. To qualify for a standard return, items must be in their original condition, accompanied by all manufacturer packaging, manuals, accessories, and unactivated warranty documentation.

## Eligible Products and Condition Guidelines
Products eligible for standard return include apparel, home goods, unopened consumer electronics, kitchenware, fitness equipment, and books. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and free of personal modifications, odors, or pet hair. All original manufacturer tags, seals, and barcodes must remain intact and securely attached.

## Opened Packaging and Inspection
We understand that customers often need to open product packaging to inspect an item. Opened packaging is acceptable for return provided that the item has not been used, damaged, or registered with the manufacturer. When repacking the item, all internal protective bags, cables, mounting hardware, and instruction manuals must be included in the return shipment. Opened products undergo physical inspection upon arrival at our fulfillment center before a return is approved.

## Defective or Damaged Products
Opened or used products that suffer from a manufacturing defect upon arrival or arrive damaged due to carrier handling are eligible for an immediate return or direct replacement within 30 days of receipt. In cases of defective or damaged merchandise, the customer is not charged any return shipping or restocking fees. Customers are requested to take clear photographs of the defect, damaged packaging, or error code to expedite the return authorization process.

## Non-Returnable Items and Exclusions
Certain product categories are strictly excluded from our standard return policy due to health, safety, and hygiene regulations:
- Perishable goods, including gourmet food baskets, beverages, and organic grocery items.
- Personal care, intimate apparel, swimwear, and hygiene products where the sanitary seal has been broken or removed.
- Customized, personalized, or engraved items tailored specifically to the customer.
- Digital downloads, software license keys, electronic gift cards, and downloadable vouchers once issued.
- Items clearly designated as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" at the time of purchase.

## Proof of Purchase Requirements
Every return must be substantiated by a verified proof of purchase. Acceptable proofs of purchase include:
- The ShopEase order number (e.g., SE-XXXX-XXXXX) visible in your account dashboard.
- The official digital order confirmation email sent at the time of purchase.
- The physical packing slip or invoice included inside the delivered parcel.
Returns cannot be accepted or processed without a verifiable ShopEase order record.

## Return Process and Instructions
To initiate a return, customers must use the self-service Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) portal:
1. Log into your ShopEase account, navigate to "Order History", select the order, and click "Request Return".
2. Choose the specific items to return and select the reason for return from the dropdown menu.
3. If the item is damaged or defective, upload up to three clear supporting photographs.
4. Once the RMA request is submitted, a prepaid return shipping label and an RMA packing slip will be generated instantly for download and emailed to you.
5. Securely pack the items inside the original shipping container or a sturdy box, insert the RMA packing slip inside the box, and affix the prepaid label to the exterior.
6. Drop off the package at any authorized carrier drop-off location within 14 calendar days of receiving the return label.
