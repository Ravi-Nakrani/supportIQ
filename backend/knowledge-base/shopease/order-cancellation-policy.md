# ShopEase Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation Window and Timing
ShopEase employs an automated fulfillment system to ensure fast dispatch of customer orders. As a result, cancellation requests are subject to strict timing criteria:
- **Self-Service Cancellation:** Customers may cancel an order directly from their account dashboard within 60 minutes of placing the order, provided the fulfillment center has not yet begun picking and packaging the items.
- Once an order transitions to "Fulfillment in Progress" or "Dispatched", automated cancellation is no longer possible.

## How to Cancel an Order
To request a cancellation within the permissible 60-minute window:
1. Log into your ShopEase account and go to the "Order History" section.
2. Locate the relevant order and check the current status banner.
3. If the "Cancel Order" button is visible and active, click it and select your reason for cancellation.
4. Confirm the cancellation prompt. An on-screen confirmation and an automated email notification will confirm that the order has been successfully voided.
If the "Cancel Order" button is disabled or missing, the order has already advanced into fulfillment processing.

## Post-Dispatch Cancellations and In-Transit Shipments
Once an order has been picked, packaged, or handed to the shipping carrier:
- An order cannot be recalled, stopped, or cancelled while in transit.
- Customers who no longer wish to keep an item that has already dispatched should accept delivery of the parcel and subsequently initiate a standard return through our self-service returns portal within the 30-day return window.
- Refusing delivery at the door is strongly discouraged, as refused packages often experience extended carrier return delays and may be subject to a $10.00 carrier return-to-sender penalty deduction.

## Cancellation Refunds
When an order is successfully cancelled within the permitted window:
- If the payment was merely held as an authorization, the hold is released immediately, and funds become accessible within 24 to 48 hours according to bank policy.
- If the payment was already captured, a full refund including all merchandise charges, sales taxes, and shipping fees is initiated to the original payment method within 24 hours.

## Merchant-Initiated Cancellations
ShopEase reserves the right to cancel an order under exceptional operational circumstances:
- **Inventory Discrepancy:** If an ordered item is identified as out of stock due to sudden warehouse inventory variance.
- **Security and Fraud Verification:** If an order triggers automated security flags or the billing information cannot be verified after 48 hours.
In all merchant-cancelled cases, the customer is immediately notified by email, and a 100% full refund is issued without delay.
