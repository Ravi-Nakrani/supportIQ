# ShopEase Payments Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
ShopEase provides a secure, friction-free checkout experience supporting a wide selection of payment options:
- **Major Credit and Debit Cards:** Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.
- **Digital Wallets:** Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal Express Checkout.
- **ShopEase Store Credit and Gift Cards:** May be applied independently or combined with an eligible credit card to cover split balances.
We do not accept cash on delivery (COD), money orders, personal checks, or direct bank wire transfers.

## Payment Security and Data Protection
Customer security is paramount at ShopEase. All transactions are processed using industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption:
- ShopEase is certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant, the highest standard in the payment card industry.
- Full card numbers and security codes (CVV) are never stored on ShopEase application servers or databases; sensitive credentials are tokenized directly with banking gateways.
- Advanced automated fraud screening is active across all incoming orders to protect shoppers against unauthorized identity theft.

## Payment Failures and Declined Transactions
In the event that an order payment is declined during checkout:
- Common causes of payment declines include billing address mismatch (AVS mismatch), incorrect expiration dates or CVV codes, insufficient funds, or automated anti-fraud bank triggers.
- When a payment is declined, the shopping cart remains preserved for 24 hours. The customer receives an alert email with a direct link to retry the transaction using a different card or alternate payment method.
- If repeated authorization failures occur, we recommend contacting the card-issuing bank to authorize charges from ShopEase.

## Duplicate Charges and Authorization Holds
Customers reviewing their online banking accounts may occasionally notice two similar charges shortly after order placement:
- When an order is placed, an immediate temporary authorization hold is requested to verify sufficient balance. This is not a duplicate charge.
- The actual capture of funds occurs only when the fulfillment center begins packaging the parcel.
- Any temporary authorization hold that was not settled will automatically drop off the account within 2 to 5 business days, in accordance with the issuing bank's hold-release policy.
- If a genuine duplicate settled charge appears on your statement after 5 business days, contact ShopEase Support with an excerpt of your statement showing both transaction IDs for an expedited release within 48 hours.

## Invoices and Payment Confirmation
Immediately following successful order placement and payment authorization:
- A digital invoice and confirmation email detailing the order summary, line items, applicable sales tax, shipping fees, and masked payment details (e.g., Visa ending in 1234) is dispatched.
- Printable PDF invoices are available on demand in the customer account dashboard under "Order Details".
