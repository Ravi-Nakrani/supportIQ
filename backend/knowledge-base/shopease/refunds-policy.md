# ShopEase Refunds Policy

## Refund Eligibility and Approval Conditions
Refunds are issued for products returned in compliance with the ShopEase Returns Policy, cancelled orders, or orders where carrier loss has been verified:
- For physical returns, refunds are contingent upon warehouse inspection. Returned parcels undergo item verification and condition assessment within two business days of arriving at our central returns facility.
- To receive a full refund, items must match the returned RMA manifest, be in acceptable physical condition, and include all bundled components.

## Refund Processing Timelines
Once a return is physically inspected and approved by our warehouse operations team:
- The ShopEase billing system initiates the refund transaction within 3 to 5 business days following inspection approval.
- An automated email notification confirming the processed refund amount and transaction reference ID is sent immediately upon initiation.
- The actual time required for the credit to appear on the customer's billing statement depends on the financial institution or card issuer:
  - Credit and Debit Cards: 5 to 10 business days depending on the issuing bank.
  - PayPal and Digital Wallets: 1 to 3 business days.
  - ShopEase Store Credit: Instant availability in the customer account immediately upon approval.

## Method of Refund and Store Credit Bonus
All approved refunds are returned to the original payment method utilized during checkout:
- If an order was settled using a credit card, debit card, or PayPal, the refund will be credited directly back to that specific account.
- In instances where the original payment card has expired or been cancelled, issuing banks automatically route the refund to the replacement account.
- Customers may alternatively opt to receive their refund in ShopEase Store Credit. When choosing Store Credit, ShopEase applies an additional 5% bonus credit to the total refunded amount. Store Credit never expires and applies automatically to future purchases.

## Shipping Fee Refunds
Shipping fees are refunded under specific conditions:
- If a return is caused by a ShopEase fulfillment error (such as receiving the wrong item, size, or color) or if the product arrives defective or damaged, the original outbound shipping fee is refunded in full.
- For voluntary returns based on customer preference, buyer's remorse, or improper fit, outbound shipping fees (including standard or express charges) are non-refundable.

## Restocking Deductions and Partial Refunds
Under standard return conditions, ShopEase does not charge restocking fees. However, partial deductions may apply in specific circumstances:
- If an item is returned with missing manufacturer accessories, power cords, adapters, or mounting hardware, a component deduction of up to 15% may be withheld from the refund.
- If an item shows signs of non-defect customer misuse, excessive physical wear, or physical alterations, a restocking fee of up to 20% may be assessed, or the item may be returned to the customer at customer expense.
