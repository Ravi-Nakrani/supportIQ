# ShopEase Product Warranty

## Standard Warranty Coverage and Duration
ShopEase stands behind the quality and reliability of every product we sell. All electronic appliances, household gadgets, personal audio equipment, power tools, and smart home devices include a standard one-year limited warranty from the confirmed date of carrier delivery. This standard manufacturer warranty protects the original purchaser against functional defects and workmanship flaws under normal indoor residential usage.

## What is Covered Under Warranty
The ShopEase standard one-year warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects present in the internal materials or physical assembly of the product.
- Mechanical and electrical component breakdown occurring during standard, intended household use.
- Internal motor failures, heating element defects, sensor malfunctions, and display screen failures not caused by external shock or physical trauma.
- Replacement parts and certified technician labor needed to restore the product to manufacturer specifications.

## What is Not Covered (Exclusions)
The limited warranty strictly excludes damages resulting from accidents, neglect, or improper operation:
- Cosmetic damage, including surface scratches, dents, paint discoloration, and standard aesthetic wear and tear.
- Accidental damage resulting from drops, liquid spills, water submersion, fire, power surges, or environmental exposure.
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs, tampering, aftermarket firmware modification, or disassembly by uncertified parties.
- Consumable parts designed to diminish over time (such as replacement batteries, blender blades, filters, and cosmetic light bulbs), unless failure is due to a defect in materials.
- Commercial or industrial usage of products designed exclusively for consumer residential applications.

## Extended Protection Plans (ShopEase Care+)
For enhanced peace of mind beyond the standard one-year warranty, customers may purchase ShopEase Care+ extended protection plans during initial checkout or within 30 days of item purchase:
- **Care+ 2-Year Plan:** Extends full mechanical breakdown coverage to two full years and includes one instance of accidental damage coverage with a zero dollar deductible.
- **Care+ 3-Year Plan:** Extends coverage to three full years, includes priority express repair service, two accidental damage replacements, and 24/7 dedicated technical concierge assistance.

## How to File a Warranty Claim
If an eligible product experiences a covered functional breakdown during the warranty period:
1. Navigate to the ShopEase Warranty Portal in your account dashboard and select "File Warranty Claim".
2. Enter the ShopEase order number, device serial number, and a detailed description of the malfunction.
3. Attach a brief video or clear photograph showing the defect or operational failure.
4. Our technical support team reviews claims within one business day. If troubleshooting fails to resolve the issue, a prepaid shipping label will be emailed for return to our diagnostic repair center.
5. ShopEase will repair the item, provide a certified factory-refurbished unit, or issue a brand-new replacement within 7 to 10 business days of diagnostic receipt.
